# design/

**Tracked in git.** Design work for Corner: mockups, the design system, screen
explorations, and anything that documents how the app should look and behave.

This folder is version-controlled on purpose. Design output here is small and
diffable — HTML mockups, SVG, tokens, notes — so its history is worth more than
the space it costs, and losing the provenance of a design decision is expensive.

Heavy binaries do **not** belong here. Store renders, video, and ad creative go
in `marketing/`, which is ignored.

See `CONVENTIONS.md` → "Marketing and design disagreement" for the reasoning and
for how this deliberately departs from the original brief.
